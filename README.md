This is a game that I am making for CSCI5999B. The theme given for this game was "Underwater". My game, "Fessh", attempts to take on the classic game of chess and transform
it into a fictional "Atlantian" version of the game that the the peoples of Atlantis are accustomed to. In this game, your soul has been dragged down to Davy Jone's locker at
Atlantis High. You make a bargain with the janitor of the school to free your soul from the locker, but in order to do so you must beat him in a game called Fessh. The first
level of the game strives to teach the player how the pieces move. The second level strives to teach the player about the dabloons mechanic. Finally, the third level is the
final "Boss Fight" style level where you must beat your opponent with everything you've learned so far.
